WHO

Start with the principal

A principal is the actor making a request: a user, role, service, or federated identity. Roles are the normal choice for workloads because their credentials are temporary.

User
A person or legacy workload
Role
Assumed, temporary access
Service
AWS acting on your behalf
02

WHAT + WHERE

Read a policy as a sentence

Allow the action s3:GetObject on arn:aws:s3:::reports/*

Every statement combines an effect, one or more actions, and resources. A narrow ARN is usually safer than a wildcard.

03

THE DECISION

Explicit deny always wins

A request begins denied. A matching allow can open the door. Any matching explicit deny closes it again, regardless of another allow.

THE HABIT

Least privilege is a loop, not a checkbox.

Grant only what the task needs, observe real usage, remove unused access, and repeat as the workload changes.
